Ballistic Sniper Design Document
(Anti-Material Rifle line)

Overview & Fantasy
The Ballistic Sniper is the pure long-range precision option in the arsenal. It rejects energy, smart targeting, and exotic effects in favor of heavy kinetic force delivered at extreme distance. It is the “anti-materiel” answer: slow, deliberate, and punishing when it connects.
It occupies the opposite end of the spectrum from the fast, chaotic weapons (Cycler, Accelerator, Rhythm Stitchers) and the exotic transform weapons (Plate Launcher, Hard-Light, Living weapons). Its fantasy is cold professionalism — the feeling of lining up a perfect shot that ends a threat before it ever reaches the team.

Reloading notes:
when reloading it reloads 1 bullet at a time, each bullet loaded is divided over what the total reload time would be.

Base Weapon: Anti-Material Rifle
Role
Long-range single-target elimination and light vehicle/elite disruption.
Core Feel
Heavy, deliberate, high-impact. Every shot should feel expensive and consequential.
Primary Fire

Bolt-action or slow semi-automatic
High damage, high stagger
Significant damage fall-off begins only at very long range
Noticeable travel time (not hitscan) so leading targets matters
Strong bullet drop at extreme distances

Secondary / Alt Fire
None on the base version (keeps the fantasy clean). Scope zoom is the main “alt” interaction.
Magazine / Resource
Low capacity (4–6 rounds). Slow reload that feels weighty. Ammo is relatively scarce compared to most primaries.
Handling

Slow aim-down-sights speed
High recoil per shot (manageable with practice)
Very stable once aimed
Movement accuracy penalty is severe while scoped

Strengths

Highest single-shot damage among non-heavy ballistic weapons
Excellent at deleting priority targets before they become a problem
Rewards map knowledge and positioning

Weaknesses

Poor close-range performance
Low rate of fire
Vulnerable while reloading or re-acquiring targets
Requires good target prioritization


Upgrade Path 1: Death Mark
Fantasy
Turns the sniper from a pure damage tool into a death-sentence applicator. You no longer just shoot enemies — you condemn them.
Mechanical Change

Primary fire now applies a Death Mark on hit (visible to the whole team).
Marked targets take a delayed explosive blast after a short fuse (≈1.5–2.5 seconds).
The delayed blast scales with how long the mark was allowed to cook (or with additional shots into the same target).
Body shots still deal normal damage + mark. Headshots deal bonus damage and shorten the fuse.

Playstyle Shift
You start thinking in terms of “mark then reposition” or “mark multiple targets and let the fuses do work.” Encourages aggressive peeking and team follow-up, because a marked enemy is a walking bomb.
Identity Notes
This is the “smart” evolution of the ballistic sniper without becoming a homing or energy weapon. It keeps the kinetic fantasy while adding a strong temporal/area component.

Upgrade Path 2: Auto Trigger
Fantasy
The rifle abandons its bolt-action soul and becomes a rapid semi-automatic or low-RPM full-auto precision weapon. Still a sniper, but now it can hold angles and pressure multiple targets.
Mechanical Change

Fire rate increases dramatically (still far slower than an assault rifle or SMG).
Damage per shot is reduced to compensate.
Recoil becomes a continuous climbing pattern rather than a single heavy kick.
Magazine size increases moderately.
Optional: a short “focus” window after ADS where the first 2–3 shots have near-perfect accuracy before bloom sets in.

Playstyle Shift
Moves the weapon from “one perfect shot” to “controlled burst pressure at long range.” Better at dealing with groups of medium-priority targets or suppressing an area while still retaining sniper range advantages.
Identity Notes
This is the volume-of-fire answer. It should still feel heavier and more deliberate than a DMR or assault rifle — never a run-and-gun weapon.

Visual & Audio Direction
Base (Anti-Material Rifle)
Long, brutalist rifle with a thick barrel, heavy muzzle brake, and a large variable-zoom optic. Recoil animation is a solid rearward punch. Firing sound is a deep, authoritative crack with a long echo.
Death Mark
Subtle visual change: the optic gains a faint red reticle glow when a mark is active. Marked enemies show a slow-burning brand or countdown glyph. The delayed blast has a distinct kinetic/explosive audio cue (not magical).
Auto Trigger
The bolt mechanism is replaced or overdriven — visible reciprocating parts, heat haze after sustained fire, and a more mechanical, rapid “thunk-thunk-thunk” report.

Design Pillars (keep these sacred)

Every shot must feel meaningful.
Mobility and close-range performance stay intentionally weak.
Upgrades transform the pacing and decision-making, not just the numbers.
It should never feel like a laser or smart weapon — kinetic impact is the identity.
